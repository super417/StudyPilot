# Local visual reference assets

The nine numbered JPG files are the user's original StudyPilot watercolor postcards, copied without cropping or repainting.

For the explicitly requested MailerBot video reproduction, the following public reference assets were saved locally on 2026-10-27:

- `animation/mailerbot-heros.riv`: https://www.mailerbot.com/rive/mailerbot-heros.riv
- `../fonts/postcards/Denim-TRIAL-Regular.woff2`: https://www.mailerbot.com/fonts/denim/Denim-TRIAL-Regular.woff2
- `../fonts/postcards/Denim-TRIAL-SemiBold.woff2`: https://www.mailerbot.com/fonts/denim/Denim-TRIAL-SemiBold.woff2
- `../fonts/postcards/DenimINK-TRIAL-Medium.woff2`: https://www.mailerbot.com/fonts/denim/DenimINK-TRIAL-Medium.woff2
- `../fonts/postcards/PanellCondensed-TRIAL-Medium.woff2`: https://www.mailerbot.com/fonts/panell/PanellCondensed-TRIAL-Medium.woff2
- `../fonts/postcards/RobotoMono-Medium.ttf`: Google Fonts Roboto Mono, medium weight.

`animation/rive.wasm` is the runtime distributed by the installed `@rive-app/canvas` package. Its package license remains in `node_modules/@rive-app/canvas`.

These origins document the local reproduction; they do not assert ownership or commercial redistribution rights for the reference site's fonts or artwork. The reference text and pricing are display content, not StudyPilot service or subscription claims.
